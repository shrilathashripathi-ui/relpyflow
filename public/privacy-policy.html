Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 9, 2025

1. Introduction

ReplyFlow ("we", "our", "us") provides an Instagram automation service that helps businesses manage and automate their Instagram interactions. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our service.

2. Information We Collect

When you use ReplyFlow, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and password when you create an account.
  • Instagram Account Data: When you connect your Instagram Business or Creator account, we access your Instagram username, profile information, media posts, comments on your posts, and direct messaging capabilities through the official Instagram API.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use our service, including automation configurations, keywords, and message templates you create.
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated statistics about your automation performance such as number of comments detected, DMs sent, and engagement metrics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain our automation service.
  • Monitor comments on your Instagram posts and reels for specific keywords you configure.
  • Send automated direct messages to users who comment on your content, on your behalf.
  • Reply to comments on your posts based on your automation rules.
  • Display analytics and performance reports for your automations.
  • Improve and optimize our service.
  • Communicate with you about your account and service updates.

4. Instagram Data Usage

We access your Instagram data through the official Instagram Graph API and Instagram Messaging API. Specifically:

  • We read comments on your posts/reels to detect keyword matches.
  • We send direct messages to commenters on your behalf using the Instagram Messaging API.
  • We reply to comments on your posts using the Instagram API.
  • We do not store Instagram users' personal data beyond what is necessary for the automation (username and comment text).
  • We do not sell or share Instagram user data with third parties.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely in our database. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Access tokens are stored securely and are only used to perform authorized actions on your behalf.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent.
  • To comply with legal obligations or law enforcement requests.
  • To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Disconnect your Instagram account at any time.
  • Revoke our access to your Instagram data through Instagram's settings.

9. Third-Party Services

Our service integrates with Meta's Instagram API. Your use of Instagram is subject to Meta's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. We encourage you to review Meta's privacy practices.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
