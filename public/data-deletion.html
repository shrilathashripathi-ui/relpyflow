Data Deletion Instructions

How to request deletion of your data from ReplyFlow

What Data We Store

When you connect your Instagram account to ReplyFlow, we store:

  • Your Instagram username and user ID
  • Access tokens for API communication
  • Automation configurations (keywords, message templates)
  • DM history and comment interaction logs
  • Analytics data (aggregated counts)

How to Delete Your Data

Option 1: Through ReplyFlow

  1. Log into your ReplyFlow account.
  2. Go to Account Settings.
  3. Click "Delete Account" to remove all your data.
  4. This will immediately delete all your stored data including connected Instagram accounts, automations, and history.

Option 2: Through Instagram

  1. Go to your Instagram Settings.
  2. Navigate to Security > Apps and Websites.
  3. Find "ReplyFlow" and click "Remove".
  4. This will revoke our access to your Instagram account.

Option 3: Contact Us Directly

Send an email to support@replyflow.app with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include your account email or Instagram username. We will process your request and delete all associated data within 30 days.

What Happens After Deletion

  • All your Instagram access tokens are immediately revoked and deleted.
  • All automation configurations, keywords, and message templates are permanently deleted.
  • All DM history and comment interaction logs are permanently deleted.
  • All analytics data associated with your account is permanently deleted.
  • Your ReplyFlow user account is permanently deleted.

Data Deletion Callback

When you remove ReplyFlow from your Instagram authorized apps, we automatically receive a callback notification and begin the data deletion process. You will receive a confirmation once the deletion is complete.

Contact

For any questions about data deletion, contact us at: support@replyflow.app
